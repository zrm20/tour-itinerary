# Glossary

## User

A person recognized by the application who may own organizations or have access to tours. Their identity in the application remains the same when their sign-in provider changes.

## Organization

A group that owns tours. An organization can have multiple owners, each with authority over its tours and tour access.

## Organization owner

A user with authority over everything within an organization, including its tours and the people assigned to those tours.

## Tour

A named collection of dated itinerary items owned by exactly one organization. A tour has no required start or end date.

## Tour admin

A user assigned to administer a specific tour. Tour admins can manage that tour and add tour members, but cannot grant tour-admin access or manage the organization.

## Tour member

A user assigned to a specific tour with read access to that tour.

## Itinerary item

A dated part of a tour's itinerary. It may be timed, such as a load-in, performance, or media event, or it may be an all-day item without an exact time. Its exact structure and categories are not yet defined.

## Tour access

Access is assigned for each tour. Being assigned to one tour does not grant access to other tours in the same organization.
